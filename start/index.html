About these tools

This site is the personal website of Dr. Vivek H. Patil, Director of Graduate Business Programs. Both tools are pilot projects led by Dr. Patil. They are not official Gonzaga University services.

For official program details, tuition, and deadlines, use the MBA pages on gonzaga.edu. If a pilot tool and an official page disagree, the official page is correct.

The MBA Concierge uses ElevenLabs, an outside service, to process conversations. Please don't share personal information such as your GU ID, email address, or Social Security number.
